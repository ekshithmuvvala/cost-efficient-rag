# Company Vacation Policy

All full-time employees accrue 15 days of paid vacation per year.
Vacation days accrue monthly, at a rate of 1.25 days per month.

Unused vacation days roll over to the next year, up to a maximum
of 5 carried-over days. Any days beyond that cap are forfeited on
December 31st.

Employees must request vacation at least 2 weeks in advance through
the HR portal, except in emergencies, which should be reported to
a manager as soon as possible.

Part-time employees accrue vacation on a pro-rated basis, calculated
as (hours worked per week / 40) multiplied by the full-time accrual rate.

# Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval.
Fully remote arrangements require VP-level sign-off and are reviewed
every 6 months.

All remote employees must be reachable during core hours, 10am-3pm
in their local time zone, and must attend the weekly team sync.
